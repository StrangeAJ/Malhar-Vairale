Risk Management

Risk Matrix

Mitigation Strategies

Supply Chain Disruptions

  • Multiple supplier relationships for critical materials
  • Strategic inventory buffers for key components
  • Regular supplier audits and performance monitoring

Technology Risks

  • Continuous R&D investment in next-gen technologies
  • Technology transfer agreements with partners
  • Patent protection for proprietary developments

Market Risks

  • Flexible production capacity planning
  • Phased investment approach tied to market demand
  • Regular market research and demand forecasting
