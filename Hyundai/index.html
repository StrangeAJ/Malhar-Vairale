Company Overview

Hyundai Motor India Limited (HMIL), established in 1996, has grown to become India's second-largest carmaker with a strong manufacturing and distribution network.

History & Presence

Operating since 1996 as a subsidiary of Hyundai Motor Company, HMIL has established itself as a key player in the Indian automotive market with a comprehensive dealer network spanning across the country.

Vision & Mission

Under the "Progress for Humanity" vision, HMIL is committed to driving sustainable manufacturing practices and delivering innovative mobility solutions to the Indian market.

EV Journey

Despite accounting for just 1.9% of FY2023 EV sales, HMIL has shown remarkable growth of 151% YoY, signaling vast potential in the Indian electric vehicle market.

Implementation Roadmap

A phased approach to implementing Hyundai's EV strategy in India from 2024 to 2030.

Phase 1: Foundation (2024-2026)

  • Finalize strategic partnerships with battery manufacturers
  • Initiate local battery assembly operations
  • Begin conversion of popular ICE models to EV variants
  • Launch premium mid-segment EV model

Phase 2: Expansion (2026-2028)

  • Build dedicated EV manufacturing plant
  • Achieve 50% localization in EV component manufacturing
  • Launch network of dedicated EV showrooms
  • Introduce additional premium EV models

Phase 3: Consolidation (2028-2030)

  • Deploy proprietary charging network across India
  • Launch full lineup of EV models across segments
  • Enhance after-sales service network for EVs
  • Implement targeted marketing in Tier 2/3 cities
